Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Chess Puzzle Trainer handles user data. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect

Chess Puzzle Trainer does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require account registration and does not collect data such as names, emails, phone numbers, or location information.

2. Local Data Storage

The app stores some information locally on your device in order to function properly. This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

  • Application settings (sound, board theme, coordinates, etc.)
  • Puzzle progress
  • Puzzle streak information

This data is used only to improve your experience while using the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Chess Puzzle Trainer does not use third-party services that collect user data. The app does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

4. Children's Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected, children can safely use this application.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future if the app introduces new features that require changes to how data is handled. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TacticMax, a chess training app by MTech Forge, handles information when you use the app. TacticMax is designed to work without account creation or direct personal profile collection, while still using limited third-party services for ads, billing, and platform features.

Applies to: Chess Puzzles: TacticMax
Developer: MTech Forge
Last updated: April 6, 2026
No account required

You can use TacticMax without signing up with your name, email address, or profile.

Progress stays on device

Settings, puzzle progress, streaks, ratings, and session history are stored locally on your device.

Google services are used

Google AdMob, consent tools, and Google Play Billing may process technical or purchase-related data under their own policies.

1. Information We Collect Directly

TacticMax does not require you to create an account or submit personal profile information such as your name, email address, home address, or phone number in order to use the app.

MTech Forge does not operate a user account system, cloud sync service, or player profile backend for TacticMax at this time.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide the app's core functionality, TacticMax stores app data locally on your device. This local storage may include:

  • App settings such as sound preferences, board theme, coordinates, vision trainer options, and onboarding choices.
  • Puzzle training data such as solved and failed attempts, puzzle IDs, ratings, hint use, and session summaries.
  • Progress data such as current rating, best rating, best streak, best rush score, and daily puzzle status.
  • Reminder and notification state, including whether daily streak reminders are enabled.
  • Review prompt state, such as whether you dismissed a review request or opened the Play Store review flow.
  • Purchase status stored locally after Google Play confirms ownership of the app's ad-free product.

Unless your device or operating system backs up app data automatically, removing the app may also remove this locally stored progress.

3. Notifications and Reminder Features

TacticMax can offer optional local reminder notifications to help you maintain your training streak or return for daily practice.

  • Notifications are optional and can be enabled or disabled in the app settings.
  • On supported Android devices, the app may request notification permission before reminders are enabled.
  • The app stores reminder state locally so it can determine whether a reminder should be scheduled.

TacticMax does not use reminders to collect contact information, build marketing lists, or create personal user accounts.

4. Advertising and Consent

TacticMax may display ads through Google AdMob. Ads are currently used in certain parts of the app for non-Pro users, including puzzle rush-related flows.

When AdMob is used, Google and its partners may automatically receive or process certain information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interaction data, diagnostics, and approximate location inferred from IP address, subject to Google's own systems and policies.

Where required, the app may present consent choices and privacy options before ads are requested. You may also have access to a Manage Consent option inside the app.

Learn more here: Google Privacy Policy

5. In-App Purchases

TacticMax offers an optional Google Play in-app purchase for an ad-free or Pro-style entitlement. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing, not by MTech Forge directly.

  • MTech Forge does not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • The app may receive purchase status information from Google Play, such as whether a purchase is owned, restored, pending, or available.
  • This purchase status may be stored locally on your device so the app can unlock the correct features.

6. Review Prompts, External Store Links, and Support

TacticMax may occasionally prompt you to rate the app after certain training milestones. If you choose to continue, the app may open the Google Play listing for TacticMax.

The app also includes links for privacy policy access and contacting the developer. When you choose to use those links, your interaction with external services such as Google Play, your email app, or your web browser will be governed by those services' own privacy practices.

7. How We Share Information

MTech Forge does not sell your personal information. TacticMax also does not maintain its own player account database for sharing or reselling user profiles.

However, information may be processed by third-party providers that support specific app features, such as:

  • Google AdMob and related consent services for advertising and ad privacy controls.
  • Google Play Billing for purchase processing and purchase restoration.
  • Platform services such as your device's notification system, email app, browser, or Play Store app when you choose to use them.

8. Children's Privacy

TacticMax is a chess training app and is not built around direct collection of personal profile information from children. We do not knowingly ask children to create accounts or submit names or email addresses inside the app.

If a child uses TacticMax, third-party services such as Google AdMob or Google Play may still process technical data according to their own policies and platform rules.

9. Security and Data Retention

Because most app data is stored locally on your device, the security of that data also depends on your device security, operating system protections, and backup settings. TacticMax does not guarantee indefinite retention of local data after app removal, device reset, or storage cleanup.

10. Changes to This Policy

MTech Forge may update this Privacy Policy if TacticMax adds new features, new third-party services, or different data handling practices. The latest version should always be posted with an updated effective date.

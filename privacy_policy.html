Privacy Policy

Chess Puzzles: TacticMax

This Privacy Policy explains how Chess Puzzles: TacticMax handles user data. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect

Chess Puzzle Trainer does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require account registration and does not collect data such as:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers

You can use the application without providing any personal information.

2. Local Data Storage

To provide core functionality, the app stores certain information locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

  • Application settings (sound, board theme, coordinates)
  • Puzzle progress and statistics
  • Puzzle streak information
  • Rating progress
  • Notification preferences

This data is used only to maintain your progress and improve your experience within the application.

3. Notifications

Chess Puzzles: TacticMax may send optional local reminder notifications to help users maintain their puzzle solving streak.

  • Notifications are completely optional.
  • Users can enable or disable notifications at any time in the app settings.
  • Notifications are generated locally on the device.
  • No personal data is transmitted to any external servers.

These notifications are used solely to remind users about their puzzle streak and are not used for advertising, tracking, or analytics purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Chess Puzzles: TacticMax uses third-party services to support app functionality. These services may collect certain information automatically.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data such as:

  • Device identifiers (including advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • App interaction data

This data is used to provide relevant ads and measure ad performance. Users may be shown personalized or non-personalized ads depending on their settings.

For more information, please refer to: Google Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers optional in-app purchases (e.g., removing ads). All payments are securely processed by Google Play.

The app does not collect or store any payment information such as credit card details. Payment data is handled entirely by Google.

5. Children's Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected, the application is safe for users of all ages.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future if new features are added that affect how information is handled. Any updates will be posted on this page.
